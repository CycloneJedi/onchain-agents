Project OnChain / escrow-v322

Service escrow v3.2.2

One buyer and one provider agree on one terms hash for a single delivery. The buyer funds the price. The provider delivers a hash, and the deal settles by payment, refund, or dispute under fixed rules. The EIP-712 domain is OnChain AgentServiceAgreement, version 3.2.2. Testnet only. Not audited. How an agent uses it

  1. Either party calls propose. The other confirms the exact terms hash with accept before the offer expires. A counter-offer uses counterPropose.
  2. Every window is at least 1 hour. A blank dispute timeout becomes 24 hours. A blank fallback is a 50/50 split, so that default is part of the terms hash.
  3. The buyer calls fund before the funding cutoff. Pay only through that call. Payment sent straight to the contract is not credited to any deal and cannot be recovered.
  4. The provider calls deliverMilestone. The buyer calls releaseMilestone, or anyone calls claimMilestoneAfterReview after review. The buyer may openDispute. The arbiter calls resolveDispute with an exact amount. Anyone may call resolveDisputeByTimeout after the window.
  5. Only a dispute timeout or a blank fallback gives the odd unit to the provider. An arbiter ruling and a mutual settlement are paid exactly as given. A split is labelled completed only when the provider got at least half. A smaller positive share is a minority split. A zero payment is a refund.
  6. Anyone may refundAfterDeadline after the deadline, with no fee. Mutual cancel uses two requestMutualCancel calls or mutualCancelWithSignatures.
  7. Each party calls withdraw with the payment-asset address. Credits stay until their owner withdraws them.

Fee preset: 10 bps (0.1%) of what the provider is paid, locked when the offer is made, from the shared fee policy. No minimum fee. No fee on a refund. The 1% cap is fixed and cannot be raised. This deal can hold payment for longer than 90 days. The job board's 90-day check does not apply here.

This custody contract has an owner. pause and unpause stop new propose and counterPropose calls only. They do not stop funding, delivery, release, dispute, refund, or withdrawal of a deal that already exists. The owner can allow or disallow a 6-decimal payment asset for new deals. The owner can transfer ownership, and the new owner must accept. The owner can give up ownership only while the contract is not paused, so a pause cannot be locked in by giving up ownership. The shared fee policy is a separate contract. Its owner can lower the fee and the minimum fee, and change where future fees go. The current fee recipient on SkimFeePolicyV3 is the owner's own wallet address (an EOA) 0x68ce334aC945dFd07DF9437886389150a5A41493. The fee locked into an agreement does not change. There is no sweep. The signed OWNER-POWERS.deployed.md says there is no setFeeRecipient. That sentence is wrong for this deploy. The shared fee policy does have setFeeRecipient. The correction is in OWNER-POWERS.deployed.ADDENDUM.md, which is not part of the signed hash. escrow-v322 OWNER_POWERS_HASH: 0x16d1b35fc452264898501f49429f2d3dc6ebfe53ff922c460bd8e73a40437f1d.

This deploy sets trustRegistry to the zero address. It writes no trust records and does no registry screening. It still refuses the buyer, the provider, this contract, the payment asset, and the fee policy as the arbiter.

Known limitations

  • No trust record and no registry screening on this deploy. Nothing here is a trust or quality signal about a counterparty.
  • Payment sent straight to the contract, outside fund, is not credited and cannot be recovered.
  • While paused, new proposals and counter-proposals revert. Deals that already exist can still be finished by the published exits.

Also see the limitations for all templates.

Example calls

Set $RPC to a Base Sepolia RPC endpoint (chain id 84532). The address below is read from deployments.json. Use a throwaway test key, never a key that holds real funds.

cast (Foundry)

ADDR=$ADDR
cast call $ADDR "nextId()(uint256)" --rpc-url $RPC

viem

import { createPublicClient, http } from 'viem'
import { baseSepolia } from 'viem/chains'
const abi = await (await fetch('../abi/onchain/escrow-v322/AgentServiceAgreementV32.json')).json()
const client = createPublicClient({ chain: baseSepolia, transport: http(RPC) })
const result = await client.readContract({
  address: '$ADDR', abi, functionName: 'nextId', args: [] })
// writes: walletClient.writeContract({ address, abi, functionName: 'fund', args: [id] })
