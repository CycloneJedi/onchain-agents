Breakaway: bounded registry loop demo

A limited test network demonstration with no real value and no legal title. It follows a fixed set of 24 practice claims through bidding, settlement, yearly assessment, missed duties, grace, cure, reclaim notice and re-auction, on Base Sepolia (chain id 84532).

Before you participate:
  • Only 24 fixed claims can be auctioned: 12, 35, 36, 60, 358, 359, 360, 383, 407, 683, 684, 707, 731, 1007, 1008, 1031, 1055, 1331, 1355, 1631, 1632, 1655, 1679, 1955.
  • Every auction (initial and repeat) uses a fixed 0.05 test ETH minimum. This is a demo restriction.
  • Each claim has a yearly work-or-pay duty: a 1% wallet-paid fee each 365.25-day anniversary, with 180 days of grace.
  • Delinquency must be recorded by a participant. Reclaim becomes eligible more than 730 days after that recorded flag, and in this demo always needs a 180-day notice that cannot be restarted.
  • Work credit is simulated: one named demonstration attestor (address: loading…) can mark work complete. Evidence review, challenges and appeals are not available.
  • Fees go to a receive-only test treasury with no withdrawal function, no administrator, and nobody who can spend from it.
  • No admin key remains after setup. No proxies or upgrade paths.
  • A public test network follows real elapsed time; the multi-year stages can only be rehearsed quickly on a local node.

The loop

  1. Anyone opens an allowed claim's auction: DemoScheduler.open(uint16).
  2. Bid with signed assent: DemoAuction.bid(uint16,uint256,bool,bytes32,bytes). Outbid bidders withdrawRefund(). The auction runs seven days.
  3. Anyone settles: DemoAuction.settle(uint16). A 25% recording fee goes to the test treasury; the rest is held by the vault.
  4. Pay the yearly duty: DemoRegistration.assess(uint16), or the attestor records work with attestAssessment(uint16).
  5. After grace, anyone may flagDelinquent(uint16); later startReclaimNotice(uint16) and reclaim(uint16). Full holder payment clears notice and delinquency.
  6. Reclaim burns the old claim, pays the keeper from its test balance, records arrears, and permits re-auction. The former holder can DemoVault.claimEscheat(uint256) within four years; after that anyone can sweepEscheat(uint256).
